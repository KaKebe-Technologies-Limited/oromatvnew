## Wording rules this text follows (so review does not bounce it)
- No "no ads" anywhere: YouTube can show its own ads inside its player, and the Privacy Policy says so.
- No "24/7 live": the Live tab shows a live programme, a recording shown at a set time, or a playlist when off air. The red LIVE badge shows for all three; a recording also carries the line "Recorded programme, shown at its scheduled time. Everyone watches together."
- Movies are written "when available": the Shows tab has a Movies filter only when the site has movies (the local database has 9 shows and no movies; check the live Shows tab).
- No YouTube / Android / Google Play names in the Apple text, and no other company names in the Apple keywords.
- The only report-handling sentence is "We aim to review reports within 24 hours." (the product owner has office hours Monday to Friday). Make sure someone really does look **[CONFIRM staffing, including weekends]**.
- The radio line is only true while the Q FM stream plays. On 18 and 19 Nov 2026 the stream host in the local setting (`host.atenimedia.com`) failed its secure (TLS) handshake from the development machine. Check the live admin setting and play it on a real phone before you submit **[CONFIRM]**. If it does not play, fix it in the admin (Streams → Channels) or delete the "LISTEN TO Q FM" paragraph, "radio" from the Apple subtitle and "qfm" from the keywords.
